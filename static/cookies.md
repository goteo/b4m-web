# **Cookies Policy**

This Cookies Policy explains how cookies are used on this platform, operated by **Platoniq Foundation**, in accordance with Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services (LSSI) and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## **1. What are cookies?**

Cookies are small text files that are stored on a user’s device when visiting a website. They are used to enable essential functionalities, remember user preferences, and collect information about how the platform is used.

## **2. What types of cookies do we use?**

### **a) Technical cookies (necessary)**

These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the platform and do not require user consent. They include:

- User login and authentication
- Session management and security
- Basic platform functionalities

### **b) Preference cookies**

These cookies allow the platform to remember user preferences, such as language or interface settings, to improve the user experience.

These cookies may be disabled by the user.

### **c) Analytics cookies**

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the platform (e.g., pages visited, time spent, navigation patterns).

We may use tools such as **Matomo or Google Analytics**, configured to minimize data collection (e.g., IP anonymization).

These cookies are only used with user consent.

### **d) Third-party cookies**

Some content on the platform may come from third-party services (e.g., embedded videos or maps). These services may install their own cookies.

We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the respective providers.

## **3. How are cookies managed?**

When accessing the platform for the first time, users are presented with a cookie banner that allows them to:

- Accept all cookies
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Configure their preferences

Users can change their cookie settings at any time via the link available in the website footer (“Cookie settings”).

## **4. Cookie duration**

The duration of cookies depends on their purpose:

- Session cookies are deleted when the browser is closed
- Persistent cookies may remain stored for a defined period unless manually deleted

## **5. Relation to personal data**

Some cookies may involve the processing of personal data.

For more information on how personal data is processed, please refer to our **Privacy Policy**.
